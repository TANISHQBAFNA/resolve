# Acme

Payments, payees, and savings.

## Button
The action that sends the money or sends a form. A large primary action for checkout on mobile lives here too.

## Button Primary
A large primary action for checkout on mobile.

## Button Secondary
A quieter action beside the primary one.

## Button Danger
A destructive action, shown as danger.

## Pay CTA
The action that pays.

## Save CTA
The action that saves.

## Switch
An on or off control. Turns one setting on or off with a flick, such as paperless statements or sign-in.

## Checkbox
Mark several items, or any extras to add. Invoices to pay together use this.

## Radio
Choose exactly one option, such as one delivery slot.

## Toast
A short message after saving, or a quick note that a payment went through and fades on its own.

## Modal
Stops and asks the person to check before they delete a saved payee or cancel a transfer. Also called a dialog.

## Alert
A red message saying a card charge failed.

## Empty Message
Shown when nothing is here yet, with a hint to add the first item.

## Avatar
The person's face in a small circle next to their name.

## Badge
A count of items in the basket, on the cart symbol.

## Quantity Stepper
How many of this item to buy, with plus and minus.

## Breadcrumb
Shows where you are in the site: home, then accounts, then the current page.

## Search Field
Look up a payee by typing part of their name.

## Text Field
A field for typed text.

## Input
A generic place to type.

## Price
The total to pay, shown with currency.

## Divider
A thin line between two sections.

## Link Button
A text-only action, such as forgot password.

## Footer Bar
The strip at the very end of the page, with legal links.

## Payment Row
One line in a list of recent payments.

## List Row
One compact row for each transaction.

## Icon Close
The mark in the corner that shuts a panel.

## Tabs
Move between sections on an account page, such as Summary and Details.

## Nav Item
An entry in the side menu for a page such as Settings.

## Promo Field
Where shoppers type the code from an email offer at checkout.

## Coupon Field
Where someone types a discount code.

## Summary Card
A card giving totals at a glance, such as the month.

## Main Card
A card that holds the main block of content.

## Tag
A small label for an order's state, such as shipped.

## Chip
Small selectable labels that filter by type, and a removable label for a chosen category.

## Header Bar
The strip across the top of the page.

## Banner
A full-width announcement.

## Legacy Banner
Retired banner. Do not use.

## Legacy Alert
Retired alert. Do not use.

## Old Price
Retired price. Do not use.
