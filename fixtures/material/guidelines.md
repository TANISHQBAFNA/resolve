# Angular Material

## Tooltip
A short hint shown when the pointer rests on a symbol.

## Slide toggle
An on or off control. Flips a setting such as dark mode or a reminder email. Also called a switch.

## Checkbox
Marks each item you want, one or more, including terms you agree to. Also called a tick box.

## Paginator
Moves to the next batch of results under a long table.

## Date picker
Chooses the first and last day of a range on a calendar.

## Snackbar
A brief note near the bottom of the screen after a draft is kept or saved. A little message that goes away by itself, including undo.

## Dialog
A box that blocks the page until the person answers. Asks them to confirm before an account is erased. A pop-up for a yes or no. Also called a dialog.

## Chip
Small selectable tokens that narrow results by category, or a removable token for a chosen keyword. A clickable label used to filter.

## Progress bar
Shows how far along an upload is, including the percent of a file transferred.

## Progress spinner
A spinning circle while the page waits for data, or an endless loading circle while a background job is still running.

## Tabs
A row of labels used to switch between parts of a settings page.

## Sidenav
A panel of page links that slides in from the left edge. Also called a navigation drawer.

## Navigation rail
Side navigation for a wide desktop layout.

## Navigation bar
Links between the main areas of a phone app, along the lower edge.

## Radio button
Choose one option out of a set, such as one shipping speed out of three.

## Select
Pick one value from a collapsed set of options, such as a country or a currency. A form value, not a menu of actions.

## Autocomplete
Type a few letters and get matching suggestions, such as a city.

## Slider
Drag to set a value along a range, such as how loud music plays, or between a low and a high price.

## Expansion panel
An area that opens to reveal more details and closes again.

## Stepper
Walks someone through account setup one stage at a time.

## FAB
A round control pinned in the corner for composing a new message.

## Mini FAB
A small floating action for a phone screen.

## Extended FAB
A floating action button with a text label.

## Tree
Shows folders inside folders that open and close.

## Table
Rows and columns you can scan, such as transactions or a grid of invoices.

## Sort
Orders one column by price from low to high.

## List
A vertical list of rows, including a compact contacts list.

## List item
One row inside a list.

## Side sheet
A panel from the right edge, used on desktop to edit filters.

## Bottom sheet
A panel rising from below, such as share options.

## Text field
Where you type an exact value, such as an amount of money.

## Form field
The form wrapped around an input, including an expense form.

## Menu
A short set of actions that opens from the three-dot overflow, such as rename and delete.

## Divider
A thin line between two groups of settings.

## Toolbar
A strip along the upper edge of the app with the page name and a back arrow.

## Button
The main action that sends the form.

## Time picker
Sets the hour and minute, such as for an alarm.

## Search bar
A field for typing a search.

## Badge
A small status mark on another part.

## Card
A surface that holds related content.

## Button toggle
A segmented control for one choice or several.

## Icon
A symbol glyph.

## Icon button
A button that shows only a symbol.

## Grid list
Tiles laid out together.

## Legacy button
Retired. Replaced by Button.
